1. Overview
Pockara is an Android anti-pickpocket utility designed to detect suspicious device extraction using on-device sensors. Core protection works locally and does not require a Pockara account.

2. Data accessed and used
Depending on the features enabled by the user, Pockara may access:
- proximity sensor data;
- ambient light sensor data;
- accelerometer and gyroscope data;
- notification permission;
- vibration;
- wake lock capability;
- biometric authentication provided by Android;
- camera permission solely to control the device torch/flash.

Sensor readings are processed locally on the device to provide anti-pickpocket protection. Pockara does not upload sensor readings to a Pockara server.

3. Local app data
Pockara stores settings, calibration profiles, local event history, runtime protection state and a local PIN verifier on the device.
The PIN is not stored in plain text.
The app does not require a proprietary online account.

4. Camera and flash
If the user enables the optional flash alarm feature, Pockara may request camera permission so Android can allow the app to control the device torch.
Pockara does not take, record, save or upload photos or videos.

5. Biometric authentication
If enabled, biometric authentication is handled by Android's biometric APIs.
Pockara does not receive or store fingerprint, face or other biometric templates.

6. Google Play purchases
Pockara Pro purchases are processed by Google Play Billing.
Pockara receives purchase state and entitlement information required to unlock paid features.
Pockara does not receive full payment-card details.
Google processes purchase information according to Google's own policies.

7. Advertising and tracking
Pockara does not display third-party advertising.
Pockara does not use the Google Advertising ID for advertising or behavioural profiling.
The current app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

8. Sharing
Pockara does not sell personal information.
The developer does not intentionally share local sensor data, PIN material, calibration data or local event history with third parties.
Google Play processes purchase and app-distribution information independently as the platform provider.

9. Backup
Pockara allows Android backup only for ordinary app settings included by the app's backup configuration.
Security PIN material, runtime protection state and local history are not intentionally included in the configured backup scope.

10. Retention and deletion
Local app data remains on the device until the user clears app storage, deletes the relevant data in the app where supported, or uninstalls the app.
Pockara does not maintain a proprietary server-side user account or server database for this data.
Google Play purchase records are retained by Google according to Google's policies.

11. Security
Pockara uses Android platform security features and local cryptographic protection for the PIN verifier.
No software or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Children
Pockara is a general device-security utility and is not specifically directed to children.

13. Theft-prevention limitation
Pockara is intended to help users notice suspicious device extraction quickly. It cannot guarantee prevention of theft, recovery of a stolen device, or personal safety.

14. Changes
This policy may be updated when Pockara features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated accordingly.
