POCKARA — GOOGLE PLAY DATA SAFETY DRAFT
Version: v0.5.0 pre-launch
Date: 25 October 2026

Use this document as the source checklist when completing Play Console.
Re-check the final APK and every SDK before submission.

CURRENT APP DATA MODEL

Developer-collected data:
- No proprietary account.
- No developer backend.
- No third-party advertising SDK.
- No third-party analytics SDK.
- Sensor readings are processed locally.
- Local PIN material remains on device.
- Local event history remains on device.
- Calibration remains on device.

Third-party platform processing:
- Google Play Billing processes purchase transactions and returns purchase/entitlement state to the app.
- Google Play and Android may independently process distribution, licensing, diagnostics or platform data under Google's policies.

DATA SAFETY WORKING ANSWERS

Does the app collect or share user data with the developer or third-party SDKs?
- Based on the current source: no app-defined collection or sharing of sensor, PIN, history or calibration data off-device.
- Billing is handled by Google Play. Verify Play Console guidance for whether any billing-related fields must be declared for the final SDK/version.

Is data encrypted in transit?
- The app does not transmit its local protection data to a Pockara server.
- Google Play Billing communication is handled by Google's SDK/services.

Can users request deletion?
- There is no Pockara online account.
- Local data can be deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
- Local history can be cleared inside the app.
- Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google.

Advertising:
- Declare NO ADS for the current version.

Account creation:
- No Pockara account creation.

Sensitive permissions / access:
- CAMERA: only for optional torch/flash control; no photos/video.
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: protection status and alarm notification.
- USE_BIOMETRIC: optional local owner authentication.
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE / SPECIAL_USE: active anti-pickpocket monitoring session.
- WAKE_LOCK: optional enhanced reliability.
- Sensors: proximity, light, accelerometer, gyroscope.
