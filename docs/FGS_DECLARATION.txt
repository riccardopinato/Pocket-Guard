POCKSECURE — FOREGROUND SERVICE DECLARATION

Foreground service type:
specialUse

Manifest permission:
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Manifest subtype explanation:
User-armed anti-pickpocket protection that continuously monitors proximity, ambient light and device motion sensors during an active protection session.

PLAY CONSOLE EXPLANATION

PockSecure is an anti-pickpocket utility. The user explicitly starts a protection session. During that session the app must continue monitoring on-device proximity, ambient-light and motion sensors while the phone is in a pocket or bag and the app is not visible. Interrupting or deferring the task would prevent the user-requested anti-pickpocket function from detecting a suspicious extraction.

The service is user-perceptible through a persistent notification that clearly states the protection state. The user can stop protection from the app. Timed Journey Guard sessions stop automatically when their selected duration ends.

PockSecure does not use this foreground service for advertising, analytics, location tracking, data synchronization or hidden background activity.
