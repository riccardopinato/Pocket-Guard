Full description:
PockSecure turns your phone sensors into a simple anti-pickpocket protection layer for travel, public transport, events and crowded places.

HOW IT WORKS
Arm protection, put your phone in your pocket or bag, and let PockSecure monitor on-device sensor signals. If a possible extraction is detected, PockSecure quickly checks whether it is you and can trigger a siren, vibration and other configured responses.
